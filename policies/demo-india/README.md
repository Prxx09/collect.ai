# Illustrative Demo Policy
Not a legally validated collections policy. All real contact and money movement is disabled.

- Borrower identity verification is required before disclosing debt/account details.
- Account balances come exclusively from the authoritative ledger snapshot.
- Negotiated terms must pass a versioned deterministic policy validator.
- Promises are confirmed only after commit and read-back verification.
- Disputes require a durable case record or an explicit failure state.
- A contact-window check must use the borrower's verified timezone and the institution's reviewed jurisdiction-specific rules.
- Human review is required whenever key facts or authorization are uncertain.
